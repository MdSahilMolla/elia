About Bhawanipur Global Campus

Previously known as NSHM Kolkata, BGC is an NAAC A+ institution with MAKAUT affiliation, building on the BGES legacy to deliver impactful higher education for the future of work.

Our programmes are designed with industry, backed by strong international partnerships and a culture of innovation. From business and health sciences to media, design, hospitality, and advanced computing, learners find pathways to thrive in dynamic careers.
